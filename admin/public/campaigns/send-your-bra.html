What happens after you send it?

A clear journey from your wardrobe to material recovery.

Every step is designed to protect your privacy and prevent the bra from returning to use as wearable intimate apparel.

01

Submit Your Request

Share your basic details through the form below. Our team confirms the next collection or dispatch step on WhatsApp.

02

Wash, Dry & Pack

Wash the bra, dry it completely, and seal it inside an opaque packet. This protects both privacy and safe handling.

03

Anonymous Registration

Once received, the parcel is logged under a collection or batch reference. No garment is publicly linked to your identity.

04

Permanent Deconstruction

Wearable structure is destroyed. Hooks, underwire, rings, adjusters, foam, elastic, and textile parts are separated.

05

Material Processing

Separated components are routed to the relevant recycling or material-recovery stream based on partner capability.

06

Reward Confirmation

After verification, your reward details and collection confirmation are shared on your registered contact information.

Important: Final recycling output depends on the garment’s fibre mix and the capability of the approved processing partner. Hivra Soft should publish only those end uses that are confirmed by the actual partner.

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers before you send anything.

Still unsure? Use the WhatsApp button and ask the Hivra Soft team directly.

Will my bra be washed and sold again?
No. The programme is not a refurbishment or second-hand resale programme. Accepted bras are permanently dismantled for material processing.
Will it be donated to another woman for wearing?
No. Under the No Rewear Promise, collected bras are not donated or supplied as wearable intimate apparel.
Can someone identify that the bra belonged to me?
The parcel is handled using a collection reference. Hivra Soft should not publish names, addresses, identifiable garment photographs, or individual participant details.
Can I send a damaged bra?
Yes, old, faded, stretched, or damaged bras can be considered, provided they are washed, completely dry, free from unsafe contamination, and properly sealed.
Why do I need to wash it before sending?
Washing and complete drying support safe, hygienic handling by collection and processing teams. Wet, mouldy, or contaminated items cannot be accepted.
Can I send a bra from another brand?
Yes. The programme can accept eligible bras from any brand and in any size.
When will I receive the 25% coupon?
The campaign team will share reward details after the parcel is received and verified. Coupon validity and product eligibility should be communicated with the issued coupon.
Will I receive my bra back if I change my mind?
Once the item is accepted into a processing batch, it cannot be returned because its wearable structure may be permanently dismantled. Contact the team before dispatch if you have any concern.

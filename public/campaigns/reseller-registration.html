FAQ
Aksar Puchhe Jaane Wale Sawaal

Koi confusion? Yahan dekho ya WhatsApp par poochho

Reseller banne ke liye minimum order kitna chahiye?
Discount tiers 20 pieces se shuru hote hain (20–49, 50–99, 100–199). Registration khud free hai — pehle register karo, phir team apni quantity ke hisaab se sab guide karegi.
Discount total order par milega ya har piece par alag-alag?
Discount tumhari total order quantity par ek hi baar calculate hota hai, har piece par alag se nahi. Jaise agar tum 25 pieces order karte ho toh poore order ki value par 15% off milega.
Shipping free hai ya charge lagta hai?
20–49 pieces ke order par ₹100/order shipping charge lagta hai. 50 pieces ya usse zyada ke order par, agar order value ₹3000+ hai, toh shipping bilkul free hai.
Marketing Kit mein kya-kya milega?
50–99 pieces aur uske upar wale resellers ko logo, product images, banners, Instagram/Facebook posts, WhatsApp catalogue PDF, product videos, reels, description aur hashtags milte hain — seedha use karne ke liye ready.
100+ pieces order karne par kya extra milta hai?
100–199 pieces wale resellers ko 30% off ke saath priority dispatch (order sabse pehle pack hota hai), naye products launch se pehle access, ek dedicated account manager aur exclusive offers jaise Buy 100 Get 5 Free bhi milte hain.
Kya WhatsApp ya Instagram se bhi sell kar sakte hain?
Bilkul! Bahut saare resellers WhatsApp Business, Instagram, Meesho aur GlowRoad se sell karte hain. Hum tumhe product images aur description bhi denge jo aap seedha use kar sako.
Kya return/exchange policy resellers ke liye bhi applicable hai?
Haan, defective products ke liye hamari return policy resellers par bhi apply hoti hai. Registration ke baad dedicated support se saari details milegi.
Registration approve hone mein kitna time lagta hai?
24–48 working hours. Hamari team form check karti hai aur WhatsApp par contact karti hai verification ke baad.
